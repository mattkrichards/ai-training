Data levels

Red · Never

No AI tool, ever
  • SSNs, government IDs
  • Bank and card numbers
  • Passwords, credentials, API keys
  • Attorney-client, litigation, legal hold
  • Trade secrets, board matters, M&A
  • Investigations, whistleblower reports

Yellow · Enterprise only

Approved enterprise tools, work account
  • Customers, orders, contacts
  • Pricing, vendor costs and contracts
  • Sales, margin, financial data
  • Employee info (no SSNs)
  • Internal communications and plans

Green · Any approved tool

Then verify the output
  • Public information
  • Industry research
  • Generic drafts, no company details
  • Training content

Ask first

  • Connecting AI to company systems, email, files, or P21
  • Letting AI act or decide on its own
  • AI that talks directly to customers
  • Legal or financial documents
  • Building, training, or fine-tuning AI
  • Synthetic media of a real person
  • Any new tool, extension, or AI feature

Something went wrong?

  1. Stop and take a screenshot.
  2. Report it right away with the disclosure form (preferred) or by email to mattk@richardssupply.com.
  3. Don't try to fix or delete it yourself.

Prompt self-reports with no data breach carry no discipline. Good-faith reporting is protected.

Disclose an unapproved tool →Email instead

The disclosure form is the preferred way to report it. Email to mattk@richardssupply.com is also accepted.

Use AI. Use approved tools. Check the data. Check the output. You own the result.
