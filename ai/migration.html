Migration plan · Personal AI accounts

Move your AI work to a company account

If you've been doing Richards Supply work in a personal AI account, it needs to move into a company account we manage. Tell us what you have, and IT will help you move it. Nothing you disclose from before the policy takes effect leads to discipline.

Why we're doing this

01

Your data stays ours

Company workspaces have contracts that keep our data out of vendor model training. Personal accounts don't.

02

Access we can manage

IT can give access, set how long chats are kept, and turn an account off when someone leaves. That's not possible with a personal login.

03

Paying isn't approving

A personal subscription paid on a company card is still a personal account. Only company-managed workspaces are approved.

Timeline

1

Disclose: now

Use the disclosure form to list every AI tool you've used for company work, including personal ChatGPT or Claude accounts and anything paid on a company card. This is how we know what to move.

2

Get your company account

Everyone has Microsoft Copilot through their M365 account. Claude Enterprise and ChatGPT Team seats are assigned by role. Your manager requests them with a use case.

3

Move your work: 30-day window

IT will announce when the window opens. Follow the steps for your tool below. IT can help with any of it.

4

Close it out by the effective date: December 1, 2026

Remove company data from personal accounts and cancel any personal subscription you used for work. After this date, using a personal account for company work is a policy violation.
