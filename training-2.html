AI & your data.

Before anything goes into an AI tool, sort it: Red, Yellow, or Green. This training shows you how, using the systems you work in every day.

Module 1 · Why data matters · 2 min

What you paste can leave the building.

Anything you type, paste, upload, or record into an AI tool is data leaving your hands. Free and personal AI tools may use it to train their models. Enterprise tools have contracts that prevent this.

A real example from our policy: uploading a vendor contract with negotiated pricing into a public chatbot to summarize it. One paste, and our pricing terms are out of our control.

Customer trust

Their data, their pricing, their orders.

Supplier agreements

Terms we've agreed to keep confidential.

Competitive edge

Margins, costs, and strategy.

Legal obligations

NDAs, privacy laws, legal holds.

Module 3 · The three levels · 6 min

Red. Yellow. Green.

Red

Never — in any AI tool, even enterprise ones
  • SSNs and government IDs
  • Bank and payment card numbers
  • Passwords, credentials, API keys
  • Attorney-client communications
  • Trade secrets and formulas
  • Board confidential matters
  • Active litigation and legal holds
  • M&A discussions
  • Investigations and whistleblower reports

Yellow

Enterprise tools only, signed in with your work account
  • Customer names, contacts, order history
  • Price lists and customer-specific pricing
  • Vendor contracts, costs, and terms
  • Sales, margin, and financial data
  • Employee info (no SSNs)
  • Internal communications
  • Strategic plans, unpublished product info
  • Internal processes

Green

Fine in any approved tool
  • Public information
  • General industry research
  • Generic drafts with no company specifics
  • Training content
  • Rewording your own writing

Health information

Never enter it without explicit approval and a signed agreement (BAA) with the vendor.

Third-party content

Paid market reports, customer documents, NDA material, and copyrighted content need permission and an approved tool.

Not sure?

Treat it as one level higher, and ask.

The 3-question check before you paste: 1) Is any of it Red? Stop. 2) Is it about our customers, vendors, employees, money, or plans? Enterprise tool, work account. 3) Otherwise, any approved tool — then verify the output.
Module 4 · Gray areas and good habits · 4 min

Strip it down before you paste.

01

Share the minimum

Paste the paragraph you need, not the whole contract or the full P21 export.

02

Anonymize people

Swap names for "Customer A" or "Employee 1" unless the tool is approved for that data.

03

Screenshots count

A screenshot of a P21 screen carries everything visible on it.

04

Notetakers hear everything

Don't run Read.ai or Granola in meetings about legal, board, or HR investigation topics.

05

Mixed data takes the highest level

One SSN in a spreadsheet makes the whole file Red.

06

Accidents happen

If Red or Yellow data lands in the wrong tool, report it right away and note what was shared.
